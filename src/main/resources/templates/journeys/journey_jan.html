My Journey - January

The Beginning: A Career Pivot


At the start of January, I made a crucial decision. To transition from freelancing to a stable, full-time job. For years, I had built my expertise through freelance projects, solving complex problems, and adapting to new challenges. However, when I began applying for jobs, I quickly realized a major obstacle: despite my experience, I had no tangible credentials or publicly available proof of my work. Since I had signed NDAs with my freelance clients, I couldn't showcase my previous projects, leaving me at a disadvantage.

I faced numerous rejections, either getting filtered out by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) or failing to progress beyond interviews. However, one interview stood out. A cybersecurity company that not only considered my application but also provided insights into its Oracle Cloud infrastructure, company culture, ongoing projects, and real-world cybersecurity incidents like DDoS attacks.

What truly inspired me was the industry veteran who interviewed me. Their depth of knowledge and passion for cybersecurity left a lasting impression, sparking my curiosity about the field. I started researching cybersecurity trends and discovered that there was a global shortage of cybersecurity professionals. Even in Malaysia, initiatives were being launched to increase the workforce due to the growing frequency of cyberattacks.

At the same time, I observed that the software development job market was becoming increasingly saturated. This realization, combined with my newfound interest in cybersecurity, led me to make a bold decision: I would fully pivot into cybersecurity and re-apply to the company that had inspired me.
