### Recovery & Response:
- **Immediate Containment:** Disconnect affected systems from the network to prevent further exfiltration or damage.
- **System Restoration:** Restore systems from known clean backups to remove any traces of the malware.
- **Forensic Investigation:** Conduct a thorough forensic investigation to assess the full extent of the infection and determine if any additional systems were compromised.
- **Incident Report:** Report the breach to relevant authorities or regulatory bodies as required, following incident response protocols.
