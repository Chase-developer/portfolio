## Findings and Recommendations

### Finding 1: **Open Network Ports and Services**
#### Identified Ports:
```bash
PORT     STATE SERVICE        VERSION
53/tcp   open  domain         Microsoft DNS
```

#### Risk Rating:
- **High**: SMB and LDAP services are exposed, increasing the attack surface for authentication-based attacks.
- **Medium**: Web service on port 80 could be susceptible to web-based vulnerabilities.

#### Recommendations:
- Enforce stronger authentication mechanisms for LDAP and Kerberos.
- Conduct security assessments on the web service hosted on IIS.
