# My Journey Timeline

---

Starting in mid-January, after encountering rejections from job hunting and being inspired by a particular cybersecurity company. This led to learning cybersecurity concepts, which was a big shift, especially with limited resources in late January. Despite the challenges, progress was made by practicing on platforms like HackTheBox in early February, solidifying the understanding of key security principles.

By mid-February, the portfolio website took shape, becoming a tangible proof of skills. Later, Oracle Cloud integration was completed, and Docker was used to streamline deployments by early March. Environmental configurations were fine-tuned to ensure smooth testing and consistent results, marking the focus on DevOps by mid-March.

Towards the end of March, registering a domain and configuring DNS records became a priority. Although Kubernetes deployment was attempted, it was halted due to resource constraints, prompting a return to Docker-Compose. The start of April was marked by improvements to the website's design and responsiveness, followed by a return to HackTheBox with a focus on Blue Team challenges. Malware analysis and threat intelligence research deepened the understanding of real-world cybersecurity issues, culminating in research on the Sandworm group and its ties to cyberwarfare.
