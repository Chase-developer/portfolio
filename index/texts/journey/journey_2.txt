## Learning with Limited Resources

To make a successful transition into cybersecurity, I knew I needed the right knowledge and credentials. However, financial constraints meant I had to be strategic about my learning journey. I discovered that Coursera offered a variety of professional certificates for a monthly fee of around RM350. Given my ability to learn quickly—a skill honed during my freelancing years—I saw this as a great opportunity to gain multiple certifications in a short period.

I enrolled in the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate to build foundational knowledge. Since the interviewer had mentioned Oracle Cloud in relation to their company, I also pursued Oracle Cloud certifications to align with their job requirements. Additionally, I enrolled in the Amazon Junior Software Developer course, which covered HTML, CSS, and JavaScript—technologies listed in the job posting.

Within a month, I had completed several certifications, including Google Cybersecurity, Oracle Cloud, and Amazon Junior Software Developer. I also took additional courses that piqued my interest, such as C++. Around the same time, I discovered the ISC2 One Million Certified in Cybersecurity campaign, which provided free training for the ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) certification. I completed the training but decided to postpone the exam until I moved to the city, where commuting to Kuala Lumpur would be more manageable.

Despite these certifications, I knew they weren’t enough. I needed practical, hands-on experience to back up my newfound knowledge.
