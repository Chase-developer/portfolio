## The Beginning: A Career Pivot

In January, I made the decision to pivot from freelancing to a stable, full-time role. For years, I had developed expertise by solving complex problems and adapting to various challenges. However, when I began applying for jobs, I quickly realized I lacked tangible credentials or publicly available proof of my work. Since I had signed NDAs with my freelance clients, I couldn't showcase past projects, putting me at a disadvantage.

Despite facing numerous rejections, either from Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) or from not advancing beyond interviews, one interview stood out. I interviewed with a cybersecurity company that not only considered my application but also provided insights into their Oracle Cloud infrastructure, company culture, ongoing projects, and real-world cybersecurity incidents like DDoS attacks.

What truly inspired me was the industry veteran who interviewed me. Their depth of knowledge and passion for cybersecurity sparked my curiosity and prompted me to explore the field further. I learned about the global shortage of cybersecurity professionals, even in Malaysia, where initiatives were being launched to address the growing frequency of cyberattacks.

At the same time, I noticed the software development job market was becoming increasingly saturated. This realization, combined with my newfound interest in cybersecurity, led me to a bold decision: I would pivot fully into cybersecurity and re-apply to the company that had inspired me.

---

### Key Actions:
- Transitioned from freelancing to pursuing a full-time job.
- Faced challenges due to a lack of tangible proof of work.
- Experienced rejections from ATS and interviews.
- Interviewed with a cybersecurity company, gaining valuable insights.
- Became inspired by an industry veteran’s passion for cybersecurity.
- Researched global cybersecurity trends and the workforce shortage.
- Realized the saturation of the software development job market.
- Decided to pivot into cybersecurity and re-apply to the inspiring company.
